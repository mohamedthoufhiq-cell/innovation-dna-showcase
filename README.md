
# 🚀 Innovation DNA
### The Student Operating System

**One student. One connected journey. Unlimited possibilities.**

Innovation DNA is an AI-powered Student Operating System designed to connect student learning, skills, achievements, projects, hackathons, and career development into one unified journey.

## 🎯 The Problem

Students use multiple disconnected platforms for learning, coding, projects, certifications, competitions, and career preparation.

Their progress and achievements remain scattered, making it difficult to understand their strengths, identify opportunities, and decide what to do next.

## 💡 Our Vision

To create one intelligent platform that understands a student's growth journey and helps them discover what to learn, build, and achieve next.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Student Dashboard** — A centralized view of student progress.
- **Innovation Passport** — A record of skills, achievements, and growth.
- **Student Intelligence Core** — Opportunity recommendations based on student profiles and evidence.
- **Learning Hub** — Learning resources and development pathways.
- **Projects Hub** — Build and showcase practical projects.
- **Hackathon Hub** — Discover innovation opportunities.
- **Career Hub** — Career preparation and guidance.
- **Resume Builder** — Present student experience and achievements.

## 🧠 Technology Stack

- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- TanStack Router
- Firebase / Firestore
- Vite
